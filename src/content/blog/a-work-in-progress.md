Every notebook starts with a blank page. This is mine.

Welcome to a small space for ideas, things to build, and lessons worth keeping. It doesn’t need to be finished to be useful. It just needs a beginning.

## Why a notebook?

A notebook gives an idea room to breathe. A thought can be a few sentences today and a more useful explanation next week. There is no need to turn every observation into a grand conclusion.

The internet has plenty of places for quick reactions. A personal website can be something slower: a place to make connections, revisit a question, or explain a small discovery carefully.

> You don’t have to know everything to share something.

That is the spirit of this space. Curiosity first. Polish later.

## What belongs here

There are three loose shelves in this notebook:

- **Building:** code, experiments, and practical ways to turn an idea into something useful.
- **Learning:** explanations, reading notes, and questions that become clearer through writing.
- **Life & thoughts:** reflections on attention, creativity, and everything between the projects.

The boundaries are intentionally soft. Often the interesting part is where two shelves overlap.

## A small beginning

The best first post is not necessarily the most impressive one. It is the one that makes the second post easier to write.

Start with a question. Describe what you tried. Write down the part that surprised you. Leave a note for the next person — or for your future self.

Thanks for stopping by. There are more pages to turn.
